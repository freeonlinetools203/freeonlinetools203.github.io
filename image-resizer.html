🎯 What is an Image Resizer?

An image resizer is a tool that changes the width and height of an image, usually measured in pixels. For example, you can take a large image measuring 4000 × 3000 pixels and make it smaller for a website, social media post, or online form.

Different websites and platforms often use different image dimensions. A photo that looks good on your computer may be too large for a profile picture or may need to be adjusted before uploading. An image resizer helps you prepare the image for the size you need.

Keep in mind that resizing and compression are not exactly the same. Resizing changes the image dimensions, while compression reduces the file size. Both can be useful when preparing images for online use.

📊 Popular Image Sizes (Social Media & Web)

Different platforms and websites use different image dimensions. Choosing a suitable size can help your images fit the space where you want to use them. Here are some commonly used sizes:

  • 1920 × 1080 (Full HD): Commonly used for desktop wallpapers, presentations, and widescreen graphics.
  • 1080 × 1080 (Instagram Square): Commonly used for square Instagram feed posts.
  • 1080 × 1350 (Instagram Portrait): A portrait image size commonly used for Instagram feed posts.
  • 1200 × 628 (Facebook Link Image): A commonly used landscape size for Facebook link previews and shared graphics.
  • 150 × 150 (Small Square): Suitable for small icons, profile previews, and compact thumbnails.
  • 820 × 312 (Facebook Cover): A commonly used desktop Facebook cover image dimension.
  • 1280 × 720 (720p): A 16:9 HD dimension commonly used for YouTube thumbnails and video graphics.

🖥️ How to Resize an Image in Photoshop

Adobe Photoshop is another option for changing image dimensions. If you already use Photoshop, you can resize an image by following these steps:

  • Step 1: Open your image in Photoshop.
  • Step 2: From the top menu, select Image > Image Size.
  • Step 3: Keep the chain link icon enabled if you want to maintain the original proportions.
  • Step 4: Enter your preferred width or height.
  • Step 5: Click OK and save or export your resized image.

Another option: If you do not have Photoshop installed, you can use our online image resizer to change image dimensions directly in your browser.

🦊 How to Resize an Image in GIMP

GIMP is a free image-editing program that includes an image resizing option. Here is how you can use it:

  • Step 1: Open your image in GIMP.
  • Step 2: Click Image > Scale Image.
  • Step 3: Enter the width and height you want. Keep the chain icon linked to maintain the image proportions.
  • Step 4: Click Scale to apply the new dimensions.
  • Step 5: Select File > Export As to save your resized image.

Both Photoshop and GIMP offer image editing features. However, if you only need to change image dimensions, an online resizer can be a convenient alternative.

📱 How to Resize Images on Mac and iPhone

You can resize images on a Mac or iPhone using built-in options or an online image resizer. The steps are a little different on each device.

  • On Mac: Open your image in Preview. Click Tools > Adjust Size, enter your preferred width and height, and save the image.
  • On iPhone: You can use the Shortcuts app or a compatible photo-editing app to change image dimensions.

Using our online tool: You can also open this page in Safari or Chrome. Upload your image, select a preset size or enter custom dimensions, and download the result.

⚖️ Image Resizing vs. Image Compression: What's the Difference?

Image resizing and image compression are two different things. Both can help when you are preparing images for websites, social media, or online forms.

  • Image Resizing: This changes the width and height of an image in pixels. For example, a 4000 × 4000 pixel image can be resized to 1000 × 1000 pixels.
  • Image Compression: This reduces the file size of an image, usually measured in KB or MB. Depending on the compression settings, the image quality may also change.

Sometimes you may need to do both. First, resize the image to the dimensions you need. Then use compression if the file is still too large for uploading or sharing.
