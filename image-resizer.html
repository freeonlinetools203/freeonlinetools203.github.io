🎯 What is an Image Resizer?

An image resizer is a tool or software that changes the dimensions of an image without losing quality. Whether you need to resize images for social media, websites, thumbnails, or print, our free tool handles it instantly. It supports 8k image resizing, custom dimensions, and preset sizes.

When you take a photo on your smartphone, the image is often large (e.g., 4000x3000 pixels). This is great for printing, but terrible for websites. Large images slow down page load times, which hurts your SEO and annoys your visitors. A resizer solves this by reducing the pixel width and height to a web-friendly size.

📊 Popular Image Sizes (Social Media & Web)

Different platforms have different requirements. Using the wrong size can result in your image being cropped awkwardly or looking blurry. Here is the ultimate cheat sheet for popular image sizes:

  • 1920x1080 (HD): Standard for YouTube thumbnails, desktop wallpapers, and full-screen presentations.
  • 1080x1080 (Instagram Square): Perfect for Instagram feed posts.
  • 1080x1350 (IG Portrait): The highest-performing format on Instagram, taking up more screen space.
  • 1200x628 (Facebook Link Post): The ideal size for sharing links on Facebook. If you get this wrong, Facebook crops the image badly.
  • 150x150 (Thumbnail): Used for profile pictures, icons, and small previews.
  • 820x312 (Facebook Cover): The standard desktop size for Facebook cover photos.
  • 1280x720 (720p): Standard HD for web videos and blog images.

🖥️ How to Resize an Image in Photoshop (Alternative Method)

If you are a professional designer, you might want to know how to resize an image in Photoshop. While it is the industry standard, it is expensive and complicated for beginners. Here is how to do it in Photoshop:

  • 1. Open your image in Photoshop.
  • 2. Go to Image > Image Size (or press Alt+Ctrl+I).
  • 3. Make sure the chain link icon (Constrain Proportions) is locked to avoid stretching.
  • 4. Enter your new Width or Height. For the best quality, choose "Bicubic Sharper (reduction)" from the dropdown menu.
  • 5. Click OK and save your image.

Why use our online tool instead? Because Photoshop costs money and requires installation. Our tool does the exact same thing in 2 seconds, for free!

🦊 How to Resize an Image in GIMP (Free Software)

How to resize an image in GIMP is a common search query for Linux users. GIMP is a free alternative to Photoshop, but its interface is complex. Here is how to do it:

  • 1. Open your image in GIMP.
  • 2. Click Image > Scale Image.
  • 3. Enter your new Width and Height. Ensure the chain icon is linked to maintain aspect ratio.
  • 4. Click "Scale".
  • 5. Go to File > Export As to save your resized image.

📱 How to Resize Images on Mac and iPhone

Many users ask how to resize an image on Mac or iPhone without downloading apps.

  • On Mac: You can open the image in Preview, click Tools > Adjust Size, and enter new dimensions. But this is tedious if you have many images.
  • On iPhone: Apple has a "Resize Image" shortcut, but it's not enabled by default. You have to download a third-party app or use a website.

Our Tool is the fastest way: Open our website in Safari or Chrome on your phone, upload your photo, click resize, and save it to your camera roll.

⚖️ Image Resizing vs. Image Compression: What's the Difference?

People often confuse these two terms. Here is the breakdown:

  • Resizing: Changes the actual dimensions (pixels) of the image. A 4000x4000 image becomes 1000x1000. This is measured in pixels (px).
  • Compressing: Reduces the file size (KB or MB) of the image while keeping the dimensions the same. This is done by removing hidden data. Our new "Compress to KB" feature does exactly this.

For the best web performance, you should do both: Resize the image to 1080px, and compress it to under 100KB.
