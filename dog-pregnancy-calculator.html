2️⃣ How Long Are Dogs Pregnant?

The most common question among dog owners is: "How long are dogs pregnant?" The answer is:

🐕 Average Dog Pregnancy: 63 days (9 weeks)

📅 Normal Range: 58 to 68 days from mating

🔬 From Ovulation: 63 days ± 1 day

📆 Weeks: Approximately 9 weeks

However, how long a dog pregnancy lasts can vary based on:

  • Breed size: Small breeds (62 days) vs giant breeds (67 days)
  • Litter size: Larger litters may deliver slightly earlier
  • Individual factors: Each dog is unique
  • Accuracy of mating date: Ovulation timing matters

3️⃣ How to Calculate Dog Due Date

Learning how to calculate dog pregnancy due date is simple. Here's the formula:

Dog Due Date = Mating Date + 63 Days

To calculate dog pregnancy due date accurately:

  1. Note the first mating date — this is the most important information
  2. Add 63 days to get the estimated due date
  3. Adjust for breed size — small breeds may deliver 1-2 days earlier, large breeds 1-2 days later
  4. Track ovulation (if possible) — for maximum accuracy, use veterinary progesterone testing

4️⃣ Dog Pregnancy Week by Week

Understanding dog pregnancy week by week helps you care for your dog at every stage. Here's the complete canine pregnancy timeline:

📅 Week 1-2 (Days 1-14)

Fertilization occurs, and embryos begin dividing. No visible signs yet. The dog may have a slight change in appetite or behavior.

📅 Week 3-4 (Days 15-28)

Implantation occurs. The embryos attach to the uterine wall. Morning sickness is possible. A vet can confirm pregnancy via ultrasound around day 25-35. Heartbeats can be detected.

📅 Week 5 (Days 29-35)

Abdominal growth begins. Nipples become pinker and more prominent. Appetite increases. Your dog may need more food and rest.

📅 Week 6-7 (Days 36-49)

Significant belly growth. Puppies are fully formed. Fetal movement may be visible. Set up a quiet whelping box. Reduce exercise and stress.

📅 Week 8-9 (Days 50-63)

Nesting behavior begins. Milk production starts. Body temperature drops below 99°F (37.2°C) 24 hours before labor. Your dog may become restless and seek a quiet place.

6️⃣ Signs of Dog Pregnancy

Common dog pregnancy signs week by week include:

  • Weeks 1-3: No visible signs. Slight behavioral changes possible.
  • Weeks 4-5: Morning sickness, appetite changes, nipple enlargement.
  • Weeks 5-6: Belly enlargement, weight gain, increased rest.
  • Weeks 7-8: Significant belly growth, fetal movement, nesting behavior.
  • Week 9: Restlessness, milk production, temperature drop.

7️⃣ Pregnancy Care Tips

  • Nutrition: Switch to high-quality puppy food during the last trimester
  • Exercise: Moderate walks, but reduce intensity in the final weeks
  • Vet Visits: Schedule regular checkups and an ultrasound
  • Whelping Box: Set up a quiet, warm area by week 7
  • Temperature Monitoring: Track rectal temperature daily from week 8
  • Emergency Plan: Have your vet's number ready for complications

8️⃣ Whelping & Delivery

When your dog's due date approaches, watch for these signs of labor:

  • Temperature drop: Below 99°F (37.2°C) — labor within 24 hours
  • Restlessness: Pacing, nesting, panting
  • Loss of appetite: Refusing food
  • Contractions: Visible abdominal contractions
  • Water breaking: Clear fluid discharge

Most dogs deliver puppies without assistance, but stay nearby in case of complications. Contact your vet immediately if labor lasts more than 2-4 hours without a puppy, or if you notice signs of distress.
