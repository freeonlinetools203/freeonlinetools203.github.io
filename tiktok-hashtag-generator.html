🔥 3. Types of TikTok Hashtags - Complete Breakdown

1. Viral Hashtags (#viral, #trending, #fypシ)

Viral hashtags are broad, high-volume tags that can help your content reach a wider audience. However, they're also highly competitive. Our generator creates viral hashtags tailored to your specific niche.

2. FYP Hashtags (#fyp, #foryou, #foryoupage)

FYP hashtags are specifically designed to signal TikTok's algorithm that your content deserves to be shown on the For You Page. These are essential for any TikTok growth strategy.

3. Trending Hashtags

Trending hashtags change daily based on what's popular on the platform. Using trending hashtags on tiktok can give your content an immediate boost.

4. SEO Hashtags

SEO hashtags are keyword-focused tags that help TikTok's search algorithm understand your content. These are crucial for discoverability in TikTok search results.

5. Niche Hashtags

Niche-specific hashtags target your exact audience. For example, #gamingcommunity, #beautytips, or #fitnessmotivation. These have lower competition but higher engagement rates.

6. Recommended Hashtags

These are supplementary hashtags that TikTok's algorithm tends to favor, including #blowthisup, #xyzbca, and #goviral.
