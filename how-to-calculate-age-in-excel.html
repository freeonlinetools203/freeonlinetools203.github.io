📊 3. How to Calculate Age in Excel Using DATEDIF (Best Method)

The DATEDIF function is the most accurate way to calculate age in Excel. It handles leap years, month-end dates, and different date formats automatically.

Step 1: Enter Date of Birth

Open Excel and enter the date of birth in cell A2 (e.g., 01/15/1990).

Step 2: Use the DATEDIF Formula

In cell B2, type:

=DATEDIF(A2, TODAY(), "Y")

This gives age in complete years.

Step 3: Full Age (Years, Months, Days)

Combine all three units for full age:

=DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"Y") & " Years, " & DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"YM") & " Months, " & DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"MD") & " Days"

Output: "36 Years, 8 Months, 2 Days"

📅 4. How to Calculate Age in Excel in DD/MM/YYYY Format

If your Excel is set to DD/MM/YYYY format (common in UK, Pakistan, India), the DATEDIF formula works the same way — but ensure your dates are entered correctly.

Step 1: Ensure Correct Date Format

Right-click the cell → Format Cells → Date → Select DD/MM/YYYY.

Step 2: Enter Date of Birth

Type your DOB as 15/01/1990 (DD/MM/YYYY).

Step 3: Use the Same DATEDIF Formula

=DATEDIF(A2, TODAY(), "Y")

Excel will automatically calculate the correct age regardless of date format.

🔄 5. How to Calculate Age Between Two Dates in Excel

Need to find age between two dates (e.g., age on a specific past or future date)? Replace TODAY() with your end date.

Formula: Age Between Two Dates

=DATEDIF(A2, B2, "Y")

Where A2 = Start date and B2 = End date.

Example: Age at End of Year

=DATEDIF(A2, DATE(2026,12,31), "Y")

Full Age Between Two Dates

=DATEDIF(A2, B2, "Y") & " Years, " & DATEDIF(A2, B2, "YM") & " Months, " & DATEDIF(A2, B2, "MD") & " Days"

🧮 6. How to Calculate Age from DOB in Excel

If your date of birth is stored as text or split across cells, use these methods:

Scenario 1: DOB Stored as Text

=DATEDIF(DATEVALUE(A2), TODAY(), "Y")

Scenario 2: DOB Split in 3 Cells

=DATEDIF(DATE(A2,B2,C2), TODAY(), "Y")

Where A2=Year, B2=Month, C2=Day.

🔙 7. How to Calculate Date of Birth from Age in Excel

Sometimes you know the age but need the date of birth. Here's how:

Formula: DOB from Age

=TODAY() - (A2 * 365.25)

Where A2 = age in years. Returns approximate DOB.

More Accurate Version

=EDATE(TODAY(), -(A2 * 12))

This subtracts age in months for precise DOB calculation.

❓ 11. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How do I calculate age in Excel using date of birth?

Use =DATEDIF(A2, TODAY(), "Y") where A2 contains the DOB. This returns age in complete years.

Q2: How do I calculate age in Excel in dd/mm/yyyy format?

Same DATEDIF formula works. Just ensure the cell is formatted as DD/MM/YYYY in Format Cells → Date.

Q3: How do I calculate age between two dates in Excel?

Use =DATEDIF(A2, B2, "Y") where A2 is start date and B2 is end date.

Q4: How do I calculate age from DOB in Excel?

Use DATEDIF with the DOB cell. If DOB is text, wrap it: =DATEDIF(DATEVALUE(A2), TODAY(), "Y").

Q5: How do I calculate date of birth from age in Excel?

Use =TODAY() - (A2 * 365.25) where A2 is the age in years.

Q6: Why doesn't DATEDIF show in Excel's formula list?

DATEDIF is a hidden function. Type it manually — it works in all Excel versions.

Q7: Can I calculate age in Google Sheets?

Yes! Google Sheets supports the same DATEDIF, TODAY, and YEARFRAC functions.

Q8: How accurate is DATEDIF for age calculation?

DATEDIF is 100% accurate — it automatically handles leap years and month lengths.
