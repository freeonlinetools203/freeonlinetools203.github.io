📘 Step-by-Step Tutorial: How to Calculate Age in Excel

Follow these simple steps to calculate age from date of birth in Excel.

1 Enter Your Data

📝 Step 1: Enter Date of Birth

Open Excel and enter the date of birth in cell A1.

Example: 01/15/1990
✅ Cell A1 = 01/15/1990

2 Use the DATEDIF Formula

📝 Step 2: Calculate Years

In cell B1, enter this formula:

=DATEDIF(A1, TODAY(), "Y")
✅ Result: 25 (years)
📝 Step 3: Calculate Months

In cell C1, enter this formula:

=DATEDIF(A1, TODAY(), "YM")
✅ Result: 6 (months since last birthday)
📝 Step 4: Calculate Days

In cell D1, enter this formula:

=DATEDIF(A1, TODAY(), "MD")
✅ Result: 15 (days since last birthday)

3 Combine All Together

📝 Step 5: Full Age Display

In cell E1, combine all formulas:

=B1 & " Years, " & C1 & " Months, " & D1 & " Days"
✅ Result: 25 Years, 6 Months, 15 Days
📝 Alternative: One Single Formula

You can also use this single formula:

=DATEDIF(A1, TODAY(), "Y") & "Y " & DATEDIF(A1, TODAY(), "YM") & "M " & DATEDIF(A1, TODAY(), "MD") & "D"
✅ Result: 25Y 6M 15D

💡 Bonus Tips

  • Use TODAY() - It automatically updates to today's date every time you open the file.
  • Replace TODAY() - Use a specific date like DATE(2026,12,31) to calculate age on that date.
  • Format Cells - Make sure your date cells are formatted as Date, not Text.
  • Leap Years - DATEDIF automatically handles leap years correctly.
  • Copy Down - Drag the formula down to calculate age for multiple people.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🔹 How do I calculate age in Excel from date of birth? +
Use the DATEDIF function: =DATEDIF(A1, TODAY(), "Y") for years, "YM" for months, and "MD" for days.
🔹 What is the easiest way to calculate age in Excel? +
The easiest way is using DATEDIF: =DATEDIF(A1, TODAY(), "Y") for years only.
🔹 Does Excel calculate age in years automatically? +
Yes, with the right formula like DATEDIF or YEARFRAC.
🔹 Can I calculate age for multiple people at once? +
Yes! Enter all dates of birth in column A, then drag the formula down for each row.
