👶 Adjusted Age Calculator: Corrected Age for Premature Babies

📅 Updated: November 2026 | ⏱️ 3 min read | ✍️ By Zain Uddin | 🩺 Medically Reviewed

What is an Adjusted Age Calculator? An adjusted age calculator (also known as a corrected age calculator) helps parents and pediatricians track the developmental milestones of a premature baby. If your baby was born early, their "chronological age" (actual days since birth) is different from their "adjusted age" (how old they would be if born on their due date). Our free preemie adjusted age calculator instantly calculates this for you.

📐 How to Calculate Adjusted Age

The formula for adjusted age is simple:

Adjusted Age = Chronological Age - Weeks Born Early

Example: If your baby is 6 months old (chronological) but was born 8 weeks (2 months) early, their adjusted age is 4 months. Our tool does this math automatically using your baby's birth date and original due date.

🛠️ How to Use This Tool

  1. Enter your baby's Date of Birth.
  2. Enter the Original Due Date (the date they were supposed to be born).
  3. Click Calculate Adjusted Age.
  4. The tool will instantly show both chronological and adjusted age in years, months, and days.

🏥 Why Adjusted Age Matters

  • Accurate Milestones: Preemies develop differently. Using adjusted age prevents false concerns about developmental delays (like rolling over or walking).
  • Medical Assessments: Pediatricians use adjusted age to evaluate growth, weight, and neurological development accurately.
  • When to Stop: According to the AAP (American Academy of Pediatrics), you should stop using adjusted age when your child turns 24 months (2 years old).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Medical Guidelines)

❓ Do you use adjusted age for vaccinations?
No. Vaccinations are always scheduled according to the baby's actual chronological birth date (not adjusted age) to ensure timely protection against infections.
❓ When should premature babies start solid foods?
Weaning and solid foods are typically evaluated using adjusted age combined with physical readiness signs. Most preemies are ready for solids around 5 to 6 months adjusted age.
❓ What is the difference between chronological and adjusted age?
Chronological age is the actual time since your baby was born. Adjusted age is the time since their original due date. It is used strictly for tracking developmental milestones.
