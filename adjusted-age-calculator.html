📐 2. How to Calculate Adjusted Age

How to calculate adjusted age:

📌 Formula for Adjusted Age

Adjusted Age = Chronological Age - (Due Date - Birth Date)

Example: Baby born 8 weeks early at 32 weeks gestation

Chronological Age = 6 months, Early = 8 weeks

Adjusted Age = 4 months

📊 9. How to Calculate Adjusted Age in Excel

You can also calculate adjusted age using Excel:

📌 Excel Formula for Adjusted Age

=DATEDIF(BirthDate, TODAY(), "d") - DATEDIF(BirthDate, DueDate, "d")

Where BirthDate = baby's birth date, DueDate = original due date
