❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🔹 What is an age difference calculator? +
An age difference calculator computes the exact time gap between two dates of birth, showing the difference in years, months, and days.
🔹 How do you calculate age difference? +
Subtract the younger person's date of birth from the older person's date of birth. The result is the age difference.
🔹 What is the acceptable age gap? +
Socially acceptable age gaps vary by culture. Many use the rule: half your age + 7 years is the minimum age for dating.
🔹 Can I calculate age difference between siblings? +
Yes! Enter both siblings' dates of birth to find their exact age gap.
🔹 Is age difference important in relationships? +
Age difference can affect compatibility, life stages, and shared interests. However, every relationship is unique.
