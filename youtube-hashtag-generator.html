❓ 1. Does YouTube Use Hashtags? (The Truth)

Many creators ask, "does YouTube use hashtags" or "should I use hashtags on YouTube" — and the answer is a resounding yes. YouTube officially supports hashtags and uses them in three important ways to help your video get discovered:

  • Clickable Links: Hashtags become clickable links that take users to a feed of similar content, increasing your chances of being discovered by new viewers.
  • Above the Title: The first 3 hashtags in your description appear prominently above your video title, acting as a quick category label.
  • Search Ranking: YouTube uses hashtags as a ranking signal to help categorize your video and show it to the right audience.

If you are wondering "do you use hashtags on YouTube", the answer is that you absolutely should. It is a free and effective way to signal to the algorithm what your video is about.

🔢 2. How Many Hashtags Should I Use on YouTube?

The question "how many hashtags should I use on YouTube" is one of the most searched topics by creators. Here's the definitive answer based on YouTube's best practices:

  • Recommended: 3 to 5 hashtags per video for optimal reach.
  • Maximum allowed: 15 hashtags per video (using more than 15 will cause YouTube to ignore all of them).
  • Best mix: 1 broad hashtag + 2–3 niche hashtags + 1 trending hashtag.

If you're wondering "how many hashtags can you have on a YouTube Short", the limit is the same (15). However, it is highly recommended to use only 3-5 tags including #Shorts to avoid looking spammy.

💡 7. Pro Tips & Troubleshooting for YouTube Hashtags

To truly master good YouTube hashtags, you need to avoid common mistakes. Here are 5 pro tips:
