❓ Does YouTube Use Hashtags?

One of the most common questions is "does YouTube use hashtags" — and the answer is a resounding yes. YouTube officially supports hashtags and uses them in three important ways:

  • Clickable links: Hashtags become clickable links that take users to a page of similar content
  • Above the title: The first 3 hashtags in your description appear above your video title
  • Search ranking: YouTube uses hashtags as a ranking signal to help categorize your video

This is why using the right hashtags matters so much. Our free YouTube hashtag generator ensures you never miss out on this critical ranking factor.

🔢 How Many Hashtags Should I Use on YouTube?

The question "how many hashtags should I use on YouTube" is one of the most searched topics by creators. Here's the definitive answer:

  • Recommended: 3 to 5 hashtags per video
  • Maximum allowed: 15 hashtags per video
  • Best mix: 1 broad hashtag + 2–3 niche hashtags + 1 trending hashtag
  • Important: The first 3 hashtags appear above your title

Now, for how many hashtags can you have on a YouTube Short — YouTube Shorts follow the same rules. Use 3–5 hashtags including #Shorts, and always include 1–2 niche-specific tags.

➕ How to Add Hashtags to YouTube Videos

Wondering how to add hashtags to YouTube videos? Follow these steps:

  1. Upload your video to YouTube Studio
  2. Open the Description field and type hashtags with # symbol
  3. Place 1–2 hashtags in the title (optional)
  4. Add tags separately in the Tags section
  5. Save and publish

For how to add hashtags to YouTube Shorts and how to add hashtags in YouTube Shorts, the process is identical.
