🔑 What is a Macro Calculator? A macro calculator is an AI-powered tool that calculates your daily macronutrient needs (Protein, Carbohydrates, Fats) based on your height, weight, age, gender, activity level, and goal. Also known as a macronutrient calculator or macro split tool, our tool helps you optimize your nutrition for weight loss, muscle gain, or maintenance.
