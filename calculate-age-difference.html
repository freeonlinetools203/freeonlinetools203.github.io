📐 3. Age Difference Formula

The age difference formula is simple:

Age Difference = Older Date of Birth – Younger Date of Birth

How to calculate age difference manually:

  • Step 1: Identify which person is older
  • Step 2: Subtract the older person's date from the younger person's date
  • Step 3: Adjust for days and months (borrow when needed)
  • Step 4: The result is the age gap in years, months, and days

💡 Example Calculation

Person 1 (Older): January 15, 1990

Person 2 (Younger): August 20, 1995

  • Years: 1995 - 1990 = 5 years
  • Months: August (8) - January (1) = 7 months
  • Days: 20 - 15 = 5 days
  • Result: 5 years, 7 months, 5 days
