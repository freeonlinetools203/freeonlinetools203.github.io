1. What Is an Image Cropper?

An image cropper is an online tool that lets you remove unwanted parts from a picture and keep only the area you want. You can use it to focus on a person, object, or important detail, improve the composition, or prepare an image for a specific purpose.

Instead of editing an entire picture, cropping allows you to select a particular area and create a new image from it. For example, you can remove empty space around a subject, make a square profile picture, or create a landscape image for a website.

Our free online image cropper lets you upload an image, choose a preset aspect ratio, or make a custom crop selection. You can adjust the selected area before cropping and download the result to your device.

What Can You Do With an Image Cropper?

  • Remove unwanted areas: Cut out unnecessary edges or background space.
  • Focus on important details: Keep the main subject clearly visible.
  • Change image proportions: Choose a square, landscape, or portrait crop.
  • Prepare images for social media: Create suitable shapes for profile pictures and posts.
  • Improve image composition: Position the subject within the selected frame.

An image cropper is useful for everyday photo editing, website images, product pictures, profile photos, and many other tasks where you need a specific part of an image.

4. Popular Image Aspect Ratios Explained

An aspect ratio describes the proportional relationship between an image's width and height. It determines whether a crop looks square, wide, or tall. Choosing the right ratio helps you prepare an image for a particular layout or platform.

Our online image cropper provides eight options: Free, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 3:2, 2:3, 4:5, and 9:16. Here is what each option means and when you might use it.

1. Free (Custom Crop)

The Free option lets you create a custom rectangular selection without following a fixed aspect ratio. Use it when you want to choose the exact area of an image yourself.

2. 1:1 (Square)

A 1:1 aspect ratio has equal width and height. It creates a square image that works well for profile pictures, product thumbnails, and square social media posts.

3. 4:3 (Standard Landscape)

The 4:3 ratio creates a landscape-shaped image that is wider than it is tall. It is commonly used for presentations, general photographs, and some display layouts.

4. 16:9 (Widescreen)

The 16:9 ratio creates a wide rectangular image. It is useful for video thumbnails, website banners, presentations, and widescreen layouts.

5. 3:2 (Classic Photography)

The 3:2 ratio is a common landscape format in photography. It can be useful for photo collections, prints, and general-purpose landscape images.

6. 2:3 (Portrait)

The 2:3 ratio creates a vertical image. It can be used for portrait photographs, posters, and other tall image layouts.

7. 4:5 (Portrait Social Media)

The 4:5 ratio creates a portrait-oriented image. It is often suitable for social media feed posts that use a taller image format.

8. 9:16 (Vertical)

The 9:16 ratio creates a tall, narrow image. It is commonly used for vertical videos, Stories, and short-form video layouts.

How to Choose the Right Aspect Ratio

Think about where you plan to use the cropped image. A square crop may suit a profile picture, a widescreen crop may work for a video thumbnail, and a vertical crop may be better for a Story or portrait layout. If none of the presets fits your needs, choose Free to make a custom selection.
