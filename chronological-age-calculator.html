📐 2. How to Calculate Chronological Age

How to calculate chronological age is a common question. The basic formula is:

Chronological Age = Current Date – Date of Birth

How do you calculate chronological age manually? The manual calculation involves three main steps:

  • Days First: Subtract the birth day from the reference day. Borrow 30 days from the month if needed.
  • Months Second: Subtract the birth month from the reference month. Borrow 12 months from the years if needed.
  • Years Last: Subtract the birth year from the remaining reference year.

💡 Example Calculation

Date of Birth: January 15, 1990

Current Date: August 19, 2026

  • Years: 2026 - 1990 = 36 years
  • Months: August (8) - January (1) = 7 months
  • Days: 19 - 15 = 4 days
  • Result: 36 years, 7 months, 4 days

📊 7. How to Calculate Chronological Age in Excel

You can also calculate chronological age using Excel:

📌 Excel Formula for Chronological Age

=DATEDIF(A1, TODAY(), "Y") & " Years, " & DATEDIF(A1, TODAY(), "YM") & " Months, " & DATEDIF(A1, TODAY(), "MD") & " Days"

Where A1 = cell with date of birth

📌 Excel Formula for Age in Years Only

=INT((TODAY()-A1)/365.25)

Where A1 = cell with date of birth
