📐 3. How to Calculate Age in Years

How do you calculate age in years? The formula is simple:

Age in Years = Current Year — Birth Year

(If your birthday hasn't passed yet this year, subtract 1)

Example 1:

  • Born: January 15, 1990
  • Current: September 2, 2026
  • Age in Years = 2026 — 1990 = 36 years

Example 2 (Birthday not passed):

  • Born: December 25, 2000
  • Current: September 2, 2026
  • Age in Years = 2026 — 2000 — 1 = 25 years
