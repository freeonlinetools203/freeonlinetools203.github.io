📊 7. Calculate Days Between Two Dates in Excel

One of the most searched questions is how to calculate days between two dates in Excel. Here are the exact formulas:

Simple Days Between Two Dates in Excel

Formula: =B1-A1

Where A1 contains the start date and B1 contains the end date. Excel returns the number of days.

Calculate Number of Days Between Two Dates in Excel (DATEDIF)

Formula: =DATEDIF(A1, B1, "D")

The DATEDIF function gives more control with units: "D" for days, "M" for months, "Y" for years.

Calculate Months Between Two Dates in Excel

Formula: =DATEDIF(A1, B1, "M")

Returns the number of complete months between two dates.

Calculate Years Between Two Dates in Excel

Formula: =DATEDIF(A1, B1, "Y")

Returns the number of complete years between two dates.

Calculate Working Days Between Two Dates

Formula: =NETWORKDAYS(A1, B1)

Excludes weekends. Optionally add holidays as a third argument.

🧮 8. Calculate Age from Date of Birth in Excel

Another highly searched question: how to calculate age from date of birth in Excel. Here are the exact formulas:

Basic Age Formula

Formula: =DATEDIF(A1, TODAY(), "Y")

Returns age in complete years.

Detailed Age in Years, Months, Days

Formula: =DATEDIF(A1,TODAY(),"Y")&" years, "&DATEDIF(A1,TODAY(),"YM")&" months, "&DATEDIF(A1,TODAY(),"MD")&" days"

Returns age in years, months, and days — perfect for medical records and official documents.

Age on a Specific Date

Formula: =DATEDIF(A1, DATE(2026,12,31), "Y")

Returns age as of December 31, 2026.

Excel Age Calculation from Birth Date (Alternative Method)

Formula: =YEAR(TODAY())-YEAR(A1)

Simple approach, but doesn't account for month/day. Use DATEDIF for accuracy.
