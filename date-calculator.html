📊 6. Date Calculations in Excel

Many users prefer Excel for date calculations. Here are the most common formulas:

Calculate Days Between Two Dates in Excel

Formula: =B1-A1

Where A1 contains the start date and B1 contains the end date. Excel automatically returns the number of days.

Calculate Difference in Dates in Excel

Formula: =DATEDIF(A1, B1, "D")

The DATEDIF function gives more control with units: "D" for days, "M" for months, "Y" for years.

Calculate Number of Working Days Between Two Dates

Formula: =NETWORKDAYS(A1, B1)

Excludes weekends and optionally holidays.

🧮 7. Calculate Age from Date of Birth in Excel

One of the most searched Excel questions: how to calculate age from date of birth in Excel. Here are the exact formulas:

Basic Age Formula

Formula: =DATEDIF(A1, TODAY(), "Y")

Returns age in complete years.

Detailed Age (Years, Months, Days)

Formula: =DATEDIF(A1,TODAY(),"Y")&" years, "&DATEDIF(A1,TODAY(),"YM")&" months, "&DATEDIF(A1,TODAY(),"MD")&" days"

Returns age in years, months, and days.

Age on a Specific Date

Formula: =DATEDIF(A1, DATE(2026,12,31), "Y")

Returns age as of December 31, 2026.

How to Calculate Age in Excel Using Date of Birth (Alternative)

Formula: =YEAR(TODAY())-YEAR(A1)

Simple approach, but doesn't account for month/day.
