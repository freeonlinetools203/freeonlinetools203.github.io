📊 1. Types of Color Palettes

  • 🎨 Analogous: Colors next to each other on the color wheel — harmonious and pleasing
  • 🔄 Complementary: Opposite colors — high contrast and vibrant
  • 🔺 Triadic: Three evenly spaced colors — balanced and rich
  • ⚫ Monochromatic: Shades of a single color — clean and elegant

❓ What is an analogous color scheme?
Analogous colors are colors next to each other on the color wheel. They create harmonious and pleasing designs perfect for nature-inspired themes.
❓ What is a complementary color scheme?
Complementary colors are opposite each other on the color wheel. They create high contrast and vibrant looks ideal for making elements stand out.
❓ What is a triadic color scheme?
A triadic scheme uses three colors evenly spaced on the color wheel. It creates balanced and rich color combinations great for bold designs.
❓ What is a monochromatic color scheme?
A monochromatic scheme uses different shades, tints, and tones of a single color. It creates clean, elegant, and professional looks.
❓ How many colors should be in a palette?
Most professional palettes use 3-5 colors. Our generator creates 5-color palettes perfect for most design projects.
❓ Can I use this for wedding color palettes?
Yes! Select the Wedding mode to generate romantic color combinations perfect for wedding themes, invitations, and decorations.
❓ What is the best color palette for websites?
For websites, use a monochromatic or analogous scheme for a professional look, with a complementary accent color for buttons and CTAs.
