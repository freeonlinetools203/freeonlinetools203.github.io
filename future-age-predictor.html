📊 3. How Future Age Calculation Works

The future age calculator works by calculating the difference between your date of birth and the future date:

  • Step 1: Enter your date of birth
  • Step 2: Enter the future date
  • Step 3: The tool subtracts your birth date from the future date
  • Step 4: The result is shown in years, months, and days

Future Age = Future Date – Date of Birth
