📊 3. Excel Formula to Calculate Age Using DATEDIF

The DATEDIF function is the most accurate Excel formula to calculate age from date of birth. It handles leap years, month variations, and end-of-month dates correctly.

Basic DATEDIF Formula

=DATEDIF(A2, TODAY(), "Y")

Where A2 contains the date of birth. This returns age in complete years.

Full Age Formula (Years, Months, Days)

=DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"Y") & " Years, " & DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"YM") & " Months, " & DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"MD") & " Days"

This gives output like "36 Years, 8 Months, 2 Days".

📈 4. Excel Formula to Calculate Age Using YEARFRAC

The YEARFRAC function returns age as a decimal number. Useful for statistical analysis and precise age calculation.

=YEARFRAC(A2, TODAY())

This returns 36.42 (age in decimal years).

For whole years only:

=INT(YEARFRAC(A2, TODAY()))

📅 5. Excel Formula to Calculate Age on a Specific Date

Need to calculate age on a specific date (like school cutoff date, insurance renewal, or tax deadline)? Replace TODAY() with DATE(year, month, day).

Formula to Calculate Age at a Certain Date

=DATEDIF(A2, DATE(2026,1,1), "Y")

This calculates age as of January 1, 2026. Replace with any date.

Example: School Admission Cutoff

=DATEDIF(A2, DATE(2026,9,1), "Y")

Age as of September 1, 2026 — perfect for school admissions.

Full Age at a Certain Date

=DATEDIF(A2, DATE(2026,1,1), "Y") & " Years, " & DATEDIF(A2, DATE(2026,1,1), "YM") & " Months, " & DATEDIF(A2, DATE(2026,1,1), "MD") & " Days"

🧮 6. Excel Formula to Calculate Age Based on Date of Birth

Sometimes your date of birth is stored as text or split across multiple cells. Here's how to handle each scenario:

Scenario 1: DOB Stored as Text

=DATEDIF(DATEVALUE(A2), TODAY(), "Y")

Use DATEVALUE to convert text dates like "15-Jan-1990" into real dates.

Scenario 2: DOB Split Across 3 Cells

=DATEDIF(DATE(A2,B2,C2), TODAY(), "Y")

Where A2=Year, B2=Month, C2=Day. Combines them into a valid date.

Scenario 3: DOB from Another Sheet

=DATEDIF(Sheet1!A2, TODAY(), "Y")

Reference another sheet by adding the sheet name before the cell reference.

❓ 10. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is the best Excel formula to calculate age from date of birth?

Use =DATEDIF(A2, TODAY(), "Y") for years, or the combined formula for years, months, and days.

Q2: How do I calculate age on a specific date in Excel?

Replace TODAY() with a specific date: =DATEDIF(A2, DATE(2026,1,1), "Y") for age as of January 1, 2026.

Q3: How do I calculate age based on date of birth if DOB is text?

Use =DATEDIF(DATEVALUE(A2), TODAY(), "Y") to convert text DOB into a real date first.

Q4: Why is DATEDIF not showing in Excel's formula list?

DATEDIF is a hidden function — you must type it manually. It works in all Excel versions.

Q5: What's the difference between DATEDIF and YEARFRAC?

DATEDIF returns whole numbers (years, months, days). YEARFRAC returns a decimal (e.g., 36.42 years).

Q6: Can I calculate age in Google Sheets?

Yes! Google Sheets supports the same DATEDIF, TODAY, and YEARFRAC functions. Formulas are identical.

Q7: How do I calculate date of birth from age in Excel?

Use =TODAY() - (A2 * 365.25) where A2 is the age in years.

Q8: How do I calculate age based on date of birth using date of birth formula?

The most common is =DATEDIF(A2, TODAY(), "Y"). For full age: combine with "YM" and "MD" arguments.

🎯 11. Pro Tips

  • Format Cells: Format result cells as "General" or "Number" — not "Date"
  • Handle Empty Cells: Use =IF(A2="","",DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"Y")) to avoid errors
  • Type DATEDIF Manually: It won't auto-complete — that's normal
  • Convert Text Dates: Use DATEVALUE() for text dates
  • Quick Today's Date: Press Ctrl + Shift + ; to insert current date statically
