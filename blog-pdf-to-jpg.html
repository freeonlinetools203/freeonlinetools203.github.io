How to Convert PDF to JPG on Mobile and Desktop

Have you ever needed to turn a PDF page into an image? Perhaps you want to share a document on social media, send a page through a messaging application, or use a PDF page in a presentation.

A JPG image is often easier to share and display than a complete PDF document. Fortunately, converting PDF files into JPG images is simple, and you don't need advanced editing skills.

In this guide, we'll explain how to convert PDF to JPG on mobile phones and desktop computers using easy methods.

What Is the Difference Between PDF and JPG?

PDF and JPG are two popular file formats, but they serve different purposes.

  • PDF (Portable Document Format): PDF files are commonly used for documents, forms, reports, books, and other files that need to maintain their layout.
  • JPG (Joint Photographic Experts Group): JPG is an image format commonly used for photographs, graphics, and digital pictures.

When you convert a PDF to JPG, each selected PDF page becomes a separate image file.

Why Convert PDF to JPG?

There are several reasons why people convert PDF documents into images.

  1. Easy Sharing: JPG images can be shared easily through messaging applications, emails, and social media platforms.
  2. Use Images in Presentations: You can insert converted PDF pages into presentations, slides, and other creative projects.
  3. View Documents Easily: Some people prefer viewing individual pages as images instead of opening a PDF reader.
  4. Upload Images to Websites: Certain websites and online platforms accept image formats but may not support PDF uploads.
  5. Save Individual Pages: If you only need a particular page from a large PDF document, converting pages into JPG images can be convenient.

Converting PDF files on a computer is also easy.

  3. Upload your PDF document.

Method 2: Use Screenshot Tools

If you only need one or two pages, you can also use your computer's screenshot feature. Open the PDF document, display the page you want, and take a screenshot. Save the screenshot as a JPG image if your screenshot application supports it, or convert the saved image into JPG format.

However, screenshots may have lower resolution than direct PDF conversion.

How to Get Better Image Quality

Sometimes converted images may look blurry or unclear. Here are some tips to improve the results:

  • Choose a suitable image resolution.
  • Use a high-quality conversion tool.
  • Avoid compressing the image too much.
  • Check the converted image before sharing.
  • Use a higher DPI setting when available.

For documents containing small text, higher resolution usually provides better readability.

Avoid uploading confidential files containing passwords, financial details, identity documents, or private information to services you don't trust. Check the website's privacy policy and file deletion practices before uploading sensitive documents.
