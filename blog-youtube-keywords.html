How to Find the Right Keywords for YouTube Videos

Do you upload videos to YouTube but struggle to get views? You might be creating useful content, but people may not be finding your videos because you're not using the right keywords.

Keywords play an important role in helping YouTube understand what your video is about. When you use relevant keywords in your video title, description, and other places, you make it easier for viewers to discover your content through search.

In this guide, we'll explain how to find the right keywords for YouTube videos and use them effectively.

What Are YouTube Keywords?

YouTube keywords are words and phrases that people type into the YouTube search bar when looking for videos.

For example, someone interested in cooking might search for:

  • Easy chicken recipes
  • How to make pizza at home
  • Quick breakfast ideas
  • Healthy food recipes

These search phrases are keywords. Using relevant keywords helps YouTube understand your video's topic and can improve its chances of appearing in relevant search results.

Why Are YouTube Keywords Important?

Finding the right keywords can help your channel in several ways.

  1. Reach the Right Audience: When your video targets relevant search terms, it has a better chance of reaching people interested in that topic.
  2. Improve Video Discoverability: People often use YouTube to find answers, tutorials, reviews, and entertainment. Relevant keywords help your videos match those searches.
  3. Understand What People Search For: Keyword research gives you ideas about what viewers want to learn or watch.
  4. Plan Better Video Content: You can use keyword ideas to discover new topics and create videos that answer common questions.

5 Easy Ways to Find YouTube Keywords

1. Use YouTube Search Suggestions

One of the easiest ways to find keyword ideas is through YouTube itself. Open YouTube and start typing a topic into the search bar.

For example, type "how to lose weight" and look at the suggested searches. You may find ideas such as:

  • How to lose weight naturally
  • How to lose weight at home
  • How to lose weight without exercise

These suggestions can help you understand what people are searching for.

2. Research Similar Channels

Look at popular channels in your niche. Pay attention to their video titles, descriptions, and topics. This can give you ideas for relevant keywords.

Don't copy other creators' content. Instead, use their topics as inspiration to create original videos.

3. Use Keyword Research Tools

Online keyword research tools can help you discover related search phrases and content ideas. Some tools also provide information about search volume, competition, and keyword trends.

Compare different keyword suggestions before choosing the best ones for your video.

4. Focus on Long-Tail Keywords

Long-tail keywords are longer and more specific search phrases.

  • General keyword: Weight Loss
  • Long-tail keyword: Easy Weight Loss Exercises for Beginners at Home

Long-tail keywords can help smaller channels target more specific audiences.

5. Study Your YouTube Analytics

YouTube Studio provides useful information about how viewers discover your videos. Check your traffic sources and search terms to understand which queries bring viewers to your channel. Use this information to plan future content.

Where Should You Use YouTube Keywords?

After finding relevant keywords, use them naturally in important places.

  • Video Title: Include your main keyword in a clear and interesting title.
  • Description: Explain what your video covers and include relevant search phrases naturally.
  • Video Content: Make sure your video actually answers the topic promised in the title.
  • Hashtags: Use a few relevant hashtags when appropriate.

Remember, adding too many keywords does not guarantee more views. Focus on useful content, clear titles, and viewer satisfaction.
