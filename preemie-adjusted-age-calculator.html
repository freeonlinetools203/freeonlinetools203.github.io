📚 3. What is Adjusted Age for Premature Babies?

Adjusted age (also called corrected age) is the age of a premature baby adjusted to account for how early they were born. It helps doctors and parents track developmental milestones more accurately.

For example, a baby born 8 weeks early at 32 weeks gestation will have a chronological age of 6 months, but an adjusted age of only 4 months. This means their development should be compared to a 4-month-old baby.

Adjusted Age = Chronological Age – (40 weeks – Gestational Age at Birth)

📐 4. How to Calculate Adjusted Age for Preemies

How to calculate adjusted age for preemies:

  • Step 1: Calculate the chronological age (from birth date to today)
  • Step 2: Calculate how early the baby was born (due date - birth date)
  • Step 3: Subtract early days from chronological age
  • Step 4: The result is the adjusted age

  • Chronological Age: 7.5 months
  • Early Days: 8 weeks = 2 months
  • Adjusted Age: 7.5 - 2 = 5.5 months
  • Result: Baby's development should be compared to a 5.5-month-old

❓ 9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ What is preemie adjusted age?
Preemie adjusted age (corrected age) is the age of a premature baby adjusted for how early they were born. It helps track developmental milestones accurately.
❓ How do you calculate adjusted age for a preemie?
Calculate the difference between the due date and birth date, then subtract that from the chronological age. The result is the adjusted age.
❓ Why is adjusted age important for preemies?
Premature babies develop differently. Adjusted age helps compare them to full-term babies of the same developmental age.
❓ When do you stop using adjusted age?
Most doctors stop using adjusted age around 2-3 years of chronological age, as babies usually catch up by then.
❓ Is this preemie age calculator free?
Yes! Our preemie adjusted age calculator is 100% free to use for all parents and healthcare providers.
❓ What is the difference between chronological and adjusted age?
Chronological age is time since birth. Adjusted age is the age based on the due date, used for tracking development.
❓ What are developmental milestones for preemies?
Preemie developmental milestones include tracking objects, smiling, rolling over, sitting, crawling, walking, and talking - all based on adjusted age.
❓ How long do you use corrected age for preemies?
Corrected age is typically used until a preemie reaches 2-3 years of chronological age.
❓ Can I use this calculator for twins or multiples?
Yes! This preemie adjusted age calculator works for all premature babies, including twins, triplets, and multiples.
❓ What is the formula for adjusted age?
Adjusted Age = Chronological Age – (40 weeks – Gestational Age at Birth in weeks).
