📷 6. How to Make Image to Scanned PDF (Look Like a Scanner)

Want your PDF to look like a scanned document? Here are pro tips to create a realistic image to scanned PDF:

  • Use high-resolution photos (at least 1200×1600 px)
  • Ensure good lighting and slight shadow at edges (mimics scanner)
  • Convert to Grayscale first using our Image Converter for authentic B&W scan look
  • Use A4 page size with "Fit to page" mode
  • Keep the image slightly tilted (1-2°) for realism

Note: If you later need to edit a scanned PDF image, we recommend converting PDF back to JPG using our PDF to JPG tool, edit, then re-convert to PDF.
