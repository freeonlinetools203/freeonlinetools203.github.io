🔥 1. What is a BMR Calculator?

A BMR calculator (Basal Metabolic Rate calculator) is a health assessment tool that estimates the number of calories your body burns while at complete rest. The basal metabolic rate calculator provides a numerical value that represents the minimum energy needed to keep your body functioning – breathing, circulation, controlling body temperature, and cell growth.

BMR accounts for 60-75% of your total daily calorie burn! Whether you're looking for a BMR calculator for men, BMR calculator for women, or want to calculate BMR and TDEE, our tool provides accurate results using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, considered the most accurate by the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics.

📌 Did You Know? Your BMR is influenced by age, gender, weight, height, and muscle mass. The more muscle you have, the higher your BMR!

📐 2. How to Calculate BMR and TDEE

How to calculate BMR and TDEE: BMR is calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula. TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) is BMR multiplied by your activity level.

📌 BMR Formula (Mifflin-St Jeor)

Men: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) + 5

Women: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) − 161

📌 TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure)

  • Sedentary (little/no exercise): BMR × 1.2
  • Light Activity (1-3 days/week): BMR × 1.375
  • Moderate Activity (3-5 days/week): BMR × 1.55
  • Heavy Activity (6-7 days/week): BMR × 1.725
  • Athlete (twice daily training): BMR × 1.9

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 7. BMR for Different Groups

BMR interpretation varies for different groups:

  • Men: Generally have higher BMR due to more muscle mass
  • Women: Typically have lower BMR, but varies with hormonal changes
  • Seniors: BMR decreases with age due to muscle loss
  • Athletes: Higher BMR due to greater muscle mass
  • Pregnant Women: BMR increases during pregnancy
  • Different Ethnicities: Some groups have different metabolic rates

❓ 10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ What is BMR?
BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the number of calories your body burns while at complete rest to maintain basic functions like breathing, circulation, and cell production. BMR accounts for 60-75% of your total daily calorie burn!
❓ How to calculate BMR and TDEE?
How to calculate BMR and TDEE: BMR is calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula. For men: (10×weight) + (6.25×height) - (5×age) + 5. For women: (10×weight) + (6.25×height) - (5×age) - 161. TDEE = BMR × Activity Factor. Our BMR calculator does this automatically for you.
❓ Which BMR formula is most accurate?
The Mifflin-St Jeor formula is considered the most accurate BMR formula by the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics. It was developed in 1990 and is more accurate than the older Harris-Benedict formula.

❓ What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?
BMR is calories burned at complete rest. TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) includes your activity level and is used for weight management goals. TDEE = BMR × Activity Factor (1.2 for sedentary, up to 1.9 for athletes).
