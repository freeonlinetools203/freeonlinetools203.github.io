🏋️ 1. What is Body Fat Percentage?

Body fat percentage is the total mass of fat divided by total body mass. It's a better indicator of health than BMI because it distinguishes between fat and muscle.

  • Essential Fat: 3-5% for men, 10-13% for women (necessary for survival)
  • Healthy Range: 10-20% for men, 18-28% for women
  • Obese Range: 25%+ for men, 32%+ for women

💪 4. How to Reduce Body Fat

  • Calorie Deficit: Eat 300-500 calories less than maintenance
  • Strength Training: Build muscle to increase metabolism
  • Cardio: 150 minutes of moderate cardio per week
  • Protein: 1.6-2.2g per kg of body weight
  • Sleep: 7-9 hours of quality sleep
  • Water: Drink 2-3 liters of water daily
