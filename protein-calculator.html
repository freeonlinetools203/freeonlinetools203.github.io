🔑 What is a Protein Calculator? A protein calculator is an AI-powered tool that calculates your daily protein requirement based on your weight, activity level, and fitness goals. Also known as a daily protein intake calculator or protein requirement tool, our tool helps you determine how much protein you need for muscle growth, weight loss, or maintenance. Whether you're tracking nutrition, meal planning, or supplementing with whey protein or protein powder, this protein calculator free tool is trusted by thousands and requires no registration.

📊 How to Use This Protein Calculator

Using our protein calculator is simple:

  1. Step 1: Enter your weight in kilograms
  2. Step 2: Select your activity level
  3. Step 3: Choose your goal (Maintain/Lose/Gain)
  4. Step 4: Click "Calculate" or press Enter
  5. Step 5: View your daily protein requirement

💡 Pro Tip: Protein is essential for muscle repair, growth, and overall health. For muscle gain, aim for 1.6-2.2g per kg of body weight.

📈 Why Protein Matters for Health

  • Muscle Growth: Protein builds and repairs muscle tissue
  • Weight Management: Protein increases satiety and metabolism
  • Recovery: Protein helps post-workout recovery
  • Immune Function: Protein supports immune health
  • Hormonal Health: Protein is essential for hormone production

✅ Protein Recommendations by Activity Level

Activity Level Protein (g/kg) Example (75kg)
Sedentary 0.8 - 1.0 g/kg 60 - 75 g
Lightly Active 1.0 - 1.4 g/kg 75 - 105 g
Moderately Active 1.4 - 1.8 g/kg 105 - 135 g
Very Active 1.8 - 2.2 g/kg 135 - 165 g
Athlete / Heavy Training 2.2 - 2.6 g/kg 165 - 195 g

🥩 Best Protein Sources

  • 🥩 Chicken Breast: ~31g protein per 100g
  • 🐟 Fish (Salmon/Tuna): ~25g protein per 100g
  • 🥚 Eggs: ~6g protein per egg
  • 🥛 Greek Yogurt: ~10g protein per 100g
  • 🌱 Lentils: ~9g protein per 100g
  • 🧀 Cottage Cheese: ~11g protein per 100g
  • 🥩 Lean Beef: ~26g protein per 100g
  • 🌱 Tofu: ~8g protein per 100g

🥤 Recommended Protein Products

  • 🥤 Whey Protein: Fast-absorbing, ideal post-workout
  • 🥛 Casein Protein: Slow-release, ideal before bed
  • 🌱 Plant Protein: Vegan-friendly protein powder
  • 💪 Mass Gainer: High-calorie protein for bulking
