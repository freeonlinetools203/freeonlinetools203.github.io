🔥 4. Zodiac Sign Elements

The 12 zodiac signs are divided into 4 elements:

  • 🔥 Fire: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius - Passionate, energetic, enthusiastic
  • 🌍 Earth: Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn - Practical, grounded, reliable
  • 💨 Air: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius - Intellectual, communicative, social
  • 💧 Water: Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces - Emotional, intuitive, nurturing

⚡ 5. Zodiac Sign Qualities

Each zodiac sign also has a quality that describes its energy:

  • Cardinal: Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn - Initiators, leaders, pioneers
  • Fixed: Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius - Stable, determined, consistent
  • Mutable: Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces - Adaptable, flexible, versatile

🌙 6. Sun Sign vs Moon Sign

Sun Sign: Your main zodiac sign based on your date of birth. It represents your core personality, ego, and identity.

Moon Sign: Based on the position of the Moon at your time of birth. It represents your emotions, intuition, and inner self.

Our zodiac sign finder gives you your sun sign. For a complete astrological profile, you would need your exact birth time and location.

🌟 Featured: Leo Zodiac Sign Personality

Leo (July 23 - August 22) is one of the most charismatic signs in the zodiac. Ruled by the Sun and belonging to the Fire element, Leos are known for their confidence, creativity, and natural leadership. They are generous, loyal, and love being the center of attention. Leos thrive when they can express themselves creatively and take charge of situations.

Leo Personality Traits:

  • Strengths: Confident, ambitious, generous, loyal, encouraging
  • Weaknesses: Arrogant, stubborn, self-centered, inflexible
  • Likes: Theater, taking risks, drama, bright colors, affection
  • Dislikes: Being ignored, facing difficult reality, not being treated like royalty
  • Lucky Number: 1
  • Ruling Planet: Sun
