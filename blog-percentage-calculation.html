How to Calculate Percentage: Simple Formulas and Tricks

Have you ever wondered how to calculate your exam percentage, discount, or test score? Percentages are used in everyday life, from shopping and school results to business calculations and financial planning.

The good news is that calculating percentages is very easy once you understand the basic formula. You don't need advanced mathematical knowledge to work with percentages.

In this guide, we'll explain what percentages mean, how to calculate them, and share some simple tricks that can save you time.

What Is a Percentage?

A percentage is a number or amount expressed as a part of 100. The word percentage means "per hundred."

For example:

  • 10% means 10 out of 100.
  • 25% means 25 out of 100.
  • 50% means 50 out of 100.
  • 100% means the complete amount.

Percentages help us compare numbers and understand proportions more easily.

Basic Percentage Formula

The most common percentage formula is:

Percentage = (Part ÷ Total) × 100

Let's understand this with an example. Suppose a student gets 400 marks out of 500.

Step 1: Divide obtained marks by total marks.
400 ÷ 500 = 0.8

Step 2: Multiply the result by 100.
0.8 × 100 = 80%

The student's percentage is 80%.

How to Calculate Percentage of a Number

Sometimes you need to find a specific percentage of an amount.

For example, what is 20% of 500?

Use this formula: Percentage Amount = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Total

Calculation: (20 ÷ 100) × 500 = 0.20 × 500 = 100

Therefore, 20% of 500 is 100.

How to Calculate Discounts

Percentages are commonly used when shopping.

Imagine a product costs Rs. 2,000 and the shop offers a 15% discount.

First, calculate the discount amount: (15 ÷ 100) × 2,000 = Rs. 300

Now subtract the discount from the original price: Rs. 2,000 - Rs. 300 = Rs. 1,700

The final price after the discount is Rs. 1,700.

Simple Percentage Tricks

Here are some easy tricks to calculate common percentages mentally.

1. Find 10%

To find 10% of a number, simply divide it by 10.

Example: 10% of 800 = 80

2. Find 50%

To calculate 50%, divide the number by 2.

Example: 50% of 600 = 300

3. Find 25%

To calculate 25%, divide the number by 4.

Example: 25% of 400 = 100

4. Find 5%

First calculate 10%, then divide that result by 2.

Example: 10% of 600 = 60. So, 5% of 600 = 30

5. Find 1%

Divide the number by 100.

Example: 1% of 900 = 9

These simple tricks are useful for quick calculations without a calculator.

Where Are Percentages Used in Daily Life?

Percentages are important in many everyday situations.

  • Education: Calculate exam results and academic scores.
  • Shopping: Understand discounts and sale prices.
  • Business: Calculate profit and loss percentages.
  • Finance: Understand interest rates and financial changes.
  • Fitness: Track progress and compare improvements.
  • Statistics: Understand survey results and data.

Learning percentages can help you make better decisions and understand numerical information.

Common Percentage Calculation Mistakes

When calculating percentages, avoid these common mistakes:

  • Forgetting to multiply the result by 100.
  • Dividing the total by the part instead of the part by the total.
  • Confusing a discount percentage with the final price.
  • Using incorrect total marks in exam calculations.
  • Mixing up percentage increases and percentage decreases.

Always double-check your numbers before using the final result.
