Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find out what year I was born?

Subtract your current age from the current year. For example, if you are 25 years old in 2026, you were born in 2001 (2026 − 25 = 2001). If your birthday has not yet happened this year, you were born one year earlier, in 2000.

Can two people of the same age be born in different years?

Yes. Two people can both be 25 years old but be born in different years, because someone whose birthday has already passed this year was born a year later than someone whose birthday is still to come.

Does this calculator account for whether my birthday has passed?

Yes. If you select your birth month and day, the calculator checks whether that date has already occurred this year and gives you one exact birth year. If you skip that step, the tool shows the two possible birth years instead.

How is my Chinese zodiac calculated?

Your Chinese zodiac animal is based on your birth year and repeats in a 12-year cycle. The calculator uses the year you were born to find your animal. If you were born in January or early February, your animal may be the one from the previous year because of the Chinese New Year.

How do I find my zodiac (star) sign?

Your star sign depends on your birth month and day, not just the year. Enter your birth month and day in the optional field above and the calculator will show your sign, from Aries through Pisces.

Is the What Year Was I Born calculator free?

Yes, it is 100% free and requires no registration, sign-up or download. It works on both mobile phones and desktop computers.

Can I use this calculator for any age?

Yes, the calculator works for ages from 0 to 120 years. Simply enter your age in whole years and it will calculate your birth year instantly.

Why does the tool show two possible birth years sometimes?

When you don't provide your birth month and day, the tool can't know whether your birthday has already passed this year. So it shows both possibilities: the year if your birthday has passed, and the year if it hasn't.
