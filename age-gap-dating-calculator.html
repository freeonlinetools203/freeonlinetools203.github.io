What is the Age Gap Dating Rule?

The age gap dating rule, also known as the "half your age + 7" rule, is a popular guideline for determining if an age difference is socially acceptable for dating.

The formula is simple: Minimum acceptable age = (Older person's age ÷ 2) + 7. If the younger person's age is equal to or greater than this number, the age gap is considered acceptable.

Our age gap dating calculator automatically applies this rule to help you check your relationship's age difference. Remember, this is just a guideline - every relationship is unique!
