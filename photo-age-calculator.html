📊 6. Factors Affecting Photo Age Estimation

Several factors can affect how AI estimates age from a photo:

  • Lighting: Poor lighting can hide facial features
  • Angle: Side profiles may be less accurate
  • Image Quality: Low resolution affects detection
  • Makeup: Can hide wrinkles and affect estimation
  • Facial Hair: Can make people look older
  • Smile: Smiling can reduce wrinkle visibility
