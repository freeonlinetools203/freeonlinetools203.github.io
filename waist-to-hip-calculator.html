❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ What is waist-to-hip ratio?
Waist-to-hip ratio (WHR) is the ratio of your waist circumference to your hip circumference. It measures fat distribution.
❓ Is this waist-to-hip ratio calculator free?
Yes! This waist-to-hip ratio calculator free tool is completely free with no registration required.
❓ What is a healthy WHR?
A healthy WHR is ≤0.95 for men and ≤0.80 for women. Lower values indicate lower health risk.
❓ How do I measure waist and hip?
Measure waist at the narrowest point (above navel) and hips at the widest point (around buttocks).
❓ Does this tool store my data?
No, this WHR calculator runs entirely in your browser. We do not store any personal information.
