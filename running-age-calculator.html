📐 3. How Running Age Calculation Works

The running age calculator works by calculating the difference between your date of birth and the current moment:

Running Age = Current Date & Time — Date of Birth

Example:

🏃 7. Age Graded Running Calculator

An age graded running calculator is different from a running age calculator. While a running age calculator shows your age in real-time, an age graded running calculator is used by runners to compare their performance against age-group standards. It calculates a percentage score based on your age and running time.
