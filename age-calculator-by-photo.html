❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🔹 Can I really calculate age from a photo? +
AI can estimate age from a photo, but it's not 100% accurate. It's a fun tool, not a replacement for exact age calculation using date of birth.
🔹 How does AI estimate age from a photo? +
AI analyzes facial features like wrinkles, skin texture, and facial structure to estimate age. It's based on training data from thousands of face images.
🔹 Is photo age estimation accurate? +
AI photo age estimation has an accuracy of about ±3-5 years. For exact age, always use date of birth-based calculators.
🔹 Is my photo stored or shared? +
No! All photo processing happens locally in your browser. We do not store or share any images.
🔹 What is the difference between chronological and photo-estimated age? +
Chronological age is your exact age from date of birth. Photo-estimated age is an AI guess based on appearance, which may differ.
