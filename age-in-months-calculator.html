📐 2. How to Calculate Age in Months

How to calculate age in months is simple. The basic formula is:

Age in Months = (Current Year – Birth Year) × 12 + (Current Month – Birth Month)

For example, if you were born on January 15, 2000, and today is August 20, 2026:

Age in Months = (2026 – 2000) × 12 + (8 – 1) = 26 × 12 + 7 = 319 Months
