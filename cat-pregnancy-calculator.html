2️⃣ How Long Are Cats Pregnant?

The most common question among cat owners is: "How long is cat pregnancy?" The answer is:

🐱 Average Cat Pregnancy: 63-65 days (9 weeks)

📅 Normal Range: 61 to 70 days from mating

🔬 From Ovulation: 63-65 days

📆 Weeks: Approximately 9-10 weeks

However, cat pregnancy length can vary based on:

  • Breed: Short-haired vs long-haired cats
  • Litter size: Larger litters may deliver slightly earlier
  • Individual factors: Each cat is unique
  • Accuracy of mating date: Ovulation timing matters

3️⃣ How to Calculate Cat Due Date

Learning how to calculate cat pregnancy due date is simple. Here's the formula:

Cat Due Date = Mating Date + 63-65 Days

To calculate cat pregnancy due date accurately:

  1. Note the first mating date — this is the most important information
  2. Add 63-65 days to get the estimated due date
  3. Adjust for breed — short-haired cats may deliver earlier
  4. Track ovulation (if possible) — for maximum accuracy

4️⃣ Cat Pregnancy Week by Week

Understanding cat pregnancy week by week helps you care for your cat at every stage. Here's the complete pregnancy cycle of cats:

📅 Week 1-2 (Days 1-14)

Fertilization occurs, and embryos begin dividing. No visible signs yet. The queen may have a slight change in appetite or behavior.

📅 Week 3-4 (Days 15-28)

Implantation occurs. Nipples become pinker ("pinking up"). Morning sickness possible. A vet can confirm pregnancy via ultrasound around day 25-35. Heartbeats can be detected.

📅 Week 5-6 (Days 29-42)

Belly begins to enlarge. Appetite increases significantly. Your cat may need more food and rest. Vet can palpate fetuses.

📅 Week 7-8 (Days 43-56)

Significant belly growth. Kittens are fully formed. Nesting behavior begins. Milk production starts. Set up a quiet queening box.

📅 Week 9-10 (Days 57-70)

Nesting behavior intensifies. Body temperature drops below 100°F (37.8°C) 24 hours before labor. Your cat may become restless and seek a quiet place.

5️⃣ Signs of Cat Pregnancy

Common cat pregnancy signs include:

  • Weeks 1-3: No visible signs. Slight behavioral changes possible.
  • Weeks 3-4: Pinking up of nipples, morning sickness, appetite changes.
  • Weeks 5-6: Belly enlargement, weight gain, increased rest.
  • Weeks 7-8: Significant belly growth, fetal movement, nesting behavior.
  • Week 9-10: Restlessness, milk production, temperature drop.

6️⃣ Early Cat Pregnancy Symptoms

Common early cat pregnancy symptoms include:

  • Pinking up: Nipples become pinker and more prominent around week 3
  • Morning sickness: Vomiting or reduced appetite in early weeks
  • Behavioral changes: Increased affection or seeking solitude
  • Weight gain: Gradual increase from week 4 onwards
  • Belly enlargement: Visible from week 5

7️⃣ Pregnancy Care Tips

  • Nutrition: Switch to high-quality kitten food during the last trimester
  • Exercise: Moderate play, but reduce intensity in the final weeks
  • Vet Visits: Schedule regular checkups and an ultrasound
  • Queening Box: Set up a quiet, warm area by week 7
  • Temperature Monitoring: Track rectal temperature daily from week 8
  • Emergency Plan: Have your vet's number ready for complications

8️⃣ Queening & Delivery

When your cat's due date approaches, watch for these signs of labor:

  • Temperature drop: Below 100°F (37.8°C) — labor within 24 hours
  • Restlessness: Pacing, nesting, panting
  • Loss of appetite: Refusing food
  • Contractions: Visible abdominal contractions
  • Water breaking: Clear fluid discharge

Most cats deliver kittens without assistance, but stay nearby in case of complications. Contact your vet immediately if labor lasts more than 2-4 hours without a kitten, or if you notice signs of distress.
