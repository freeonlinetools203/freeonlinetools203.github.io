📌 1. What is an Image Converter?

An image converter is a tool that changes image files from one format to another — for example, convertir imagen a jpg, PNG to WEBP, or convert color image to black and white. Different formats serve different purposes:

  • PNG: Best for transparent backgrounds & logos
  • JPG/JPEG: Best for photos & web images (smaller size)
  • WEBP: Modern format with superior compression for websites
  • BMP: Uncompressed bitmap for maximum quality
  • Grayscale (B&W): Artistic black & white effect

Our free image converter online handles all these formats with a single click, side-by-side preview, and full quality control.
