🎯 1. What is a QR Code Generator?

A QR code generator is a software tool that takes data and encodes it into a two-dimensional barcode that can be scanned by smartphones. QR stands for "Quick Response," and these codes can store everything from a simple website URL to complex contact information (vCard), Wi-Fi credentials, and even PDF documents.

💡 8. Tips & Tricks for Best Results

  • Test Before Printing: Always scan your QR code with multiple devices (iOS and Android) before printing it on physical materials.
  • Maintain High Contrast: Use dark colors for the QR code and a light background. Avoid inverted colors (light code on dark background) as some scanners struggle with them.
  • Add a Call to Action (CTA): Tell users what to expect. Instead of just a QR code, add text like "Scan to view our menu" or "Scan to save my contact."
  • Choose the Right Size: For print materials, ensure the QR code is at least 2 x 2 cm. For billboards, make it large enough to be scanned from a distance.
  • Use Dynamic QR Codes (if needed): While our tool creates static codes (which never expire), understand that dynamic codes allow you to change the destination URL later.
  • Common Mistake: Don't place a QR code in a spot with no internet connectivity. Users need a stable connection to scan and load the content.
