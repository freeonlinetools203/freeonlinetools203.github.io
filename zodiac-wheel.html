🌟 2. Western Zodiac Signs (12 Signs)

The Western zodiac wheel is based on the tropical zodiac, dividing the sky into 12 equal parts. Each sign has unique traits:

  • ♈ Aries (Mar 21 – Apr 19): Bold, confident, adventurous
  • ♉ Taurus (Apr 20 – May 20): Reliable, patient, practical
  • ♊ Gemini (May 21 – Jun 20): Adaptable, curious, communicative
  • ♋ Cancer (Jun 21 – Jul 22): Nurturing, emotional, intuitive
  • ♌ Leo (Jul 23 – Aug 22): Confident, generous, charismatic
  • ♍ Virgo (Aug 23 – Sep 22): Analytical, practical, detail-oriented
  • ♎ Libra (Sep 23 – Oct 22): Diplomatic, fair, social
  • ♏ Scorpio (Oct 23 – Nov 21): Passionate, determined, secretive
  • ♐ Sagittarius (Nov 22 – Dec 21): Adventurous, optimistic, honest
  • ♑ Capricorn (Dec 22 – Jan 19): Disciplined, ambitious, practical
  • ♒ Aquarius (Jan 20 – Feb 18): Innovative, intellectual, independent
  • ♓ Pisces (Feb 19 – Mar 20): Intuitive, artistic, empathetic

🐉 3. Chinese Zodiac Wheel (12 Animals)

The chinese zodiac wheel follows a 12-year cycle. Each year is associated with one animal and its characteristics:

  • 🐀 Rat: Quick-witted, adaptable, charming
  • 🐂 Ox: Reliable, hardworking, patient
  • 🐅 Tiger: Courageous, competitive, unpredictable
  • 🐇 Rabbit: Gentle, compassionate, artistic
  • 🐉 Dragon: Confident, charismatic, energetic
  • 🐍 Snake: Intuitive, wise, mysterious
  • 🐎 Horse: Energetic, independent, adventurous
  • 🐐 Goat: Creative, kind, empathetic
  • 🐒 Monkey: Intelligent, playful, innovative
  • 🐓 Rooster: Observant, honest, hardworking
  • 🐕 Dog: Loyal, protective, honest
  • 🐖 Pig: Generous, compassionate, diligent

🔥 4. Elements Wheel

The elemental zodiac wheel with elements represents the fundamental forces of nature:

  • 🔥 Fire: Passion, energy, creativity (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)
  • 💧 Water: Emotion, intuition, healing (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces)
  • 🌬️ Air: Intellect, communication, social (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius)
  • 🌍 Earth: Practicality, stability, reliability (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn)
  • ✨ Spirit: Divine connection, higher purpose
  • ⭐ Aether: Cosmic energy, universal consciousness
