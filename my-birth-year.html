📐 3. How to Find My Birth Year from My Age

How do I find my birth year from my age? The formula is simple:

Birth Year = Current Year — Age

(If your birthday hasn't passed yet this year, subtract 1)

Example 1:

  • Age: 25
  • Current Year: 2026
  • Birth Year = 2026 — 25 = 2001

Example 2 (Birthday not passed):

  • Age: 25 (birthday in December)
  • Current Year: 2026
  • Birth Year = 2026 — 25 — 1 = 2000
