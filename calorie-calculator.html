📐 2. How to Calculate Daily Calorie Needs

How to calculate daily calorie needs: Your daily calorie needs are determined by your BMR (calories burned at rest) multiplied by your activity level. This gives you your TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure).

📌 BMR Formula (Mifflin-St Jeor)

Men: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) + 5

Women: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) − 161

📌 TDEE Formula

TDEE = BMR × Activity Factor

  • Sedentary (little/no exercise): BMR × 1.2
  • Light Activity (1-3 days/week): BMR × 1.375
  • Moderate Activity (3-5 days/week): BMR × 1.55
  • Very Active (6-7 days/week): BMR × 1.725
  • Extra Active (athlete/physical job): BMR × 1.9
