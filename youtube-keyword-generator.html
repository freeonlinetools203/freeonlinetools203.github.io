🏷️ 4. YouTube Channel Keywords

Channel keywords YouTube are descriptive words or phrases that tell YouTube what your channel is about. They are different from video keywords — channel keywords apply to your entire channel and help YouTube recommend your content to the right audience.

What Does "Channel Keywords" Mean on YouTube?

What does channel keywords mean on YouTube? Channel keywords are metadata added in your YouTube Studio (Settings → Channel → Basic Info). They help YouTube's algorithm understand your channel's topic, niche, and target audience. Unlike video tags, channel keywords apply to your whole channel.

Channel Keywords YouTube Meaning (Explained)

The channel keywords YouTube meaning is simple: they're tags that describe your channel's focus. Example — for a gaming channel, channel keywords could be: "gaming, game reviews, esports, walkthroughs, gaming tips, let's play."

🔢 6. How Many Keywords YouTube? (Channel & Video Limits)

How many channel keywords YouTube? and how many keywords YouTube allows per video? Here are the exact limits:

  • Channel Keywords Limit: YouTube allows up to 500 characters total for channel keywords. That's typically 5-15 comma-separated keywords
  • Video Tags Limit: YouTube allows up to 500 characters for video tags too
  • Best Practice: Use 5-10 keywords per video and 5-15 channel keywords
  • Do NOT overstuff: Quality matters more than quantity — use only relevant keywords

📊 7. Long-Tail Keywords for YouTube

Long-tail keywords are specific phrases (3-5 words) with lower competition and higher conversion. Examples:

  • Short: "gaming" → Long-Tail: "best gaming setup for beginners"
  • Short: "cooking" → Long-Tail: "easy chicken recipes for dinner"
  • Short: "SEO" → Long-Tail: "YouTube SEO tips for beginners"
  • Short: "fitness" → Long-Tail: "home workout for beginners no equipment"

🚫 8. How to Block Keywords in YouTube Search

Many users search "how to block keywords in YouTube search". Here's the truth:

  • Official Method: YouTube doesn't natively block keywords in search results
  • Content Filters: Use YouTube Kids or Restricted Mode to filter content
  • Blocked Words: As a creator, you can add "blocked words" in YouTube Studio → Settings → Community → Blocked Words (for comments)
  • Third-Party Tools: Browser extensions like "Keyword Block" can filter search results on desktop
  • Report Content: For inappropriate content, use YouTube's report feature
