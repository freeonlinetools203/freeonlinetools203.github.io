📊 8. Long-Tail Keywords for YouTube

Long-tail keywords are specific phrases (3-5 words) that have lower competition and higher conversion rates. Examples:

  • Short Keyword: "gaming" (high competition)
  • Long-Tail Keyword: "best gaming setup for beginners" (low competition)
  • Short Keyword: "cooking" (high competition)
  • Long-Tail Keyword: "easy chicken recipes for dinner" (low competition)
  • Short Keyword: "SEO" (high competition)
  • Long-Tail Keyword: "YouTube SEO tips for beginners" (low competition)

🏷️ 9. YouTube Channel Keywords

Channel keywords help YouTube understand what your channel is about. These are different from video keywords:

  • Channel Keywords: Added in YouTube Studio under "Basic Info"
  • Video Keywords: Added to individual videos for search optimization
  • Best Practice: Use 5-10 channel keywords that describe your niche
  • Example: For a gaming channel: "gaming, walkthroughs, game reviews, esports, gaming tips"

📝 10. How to Add Keywords to YouTube Video

How to add keywords to YouTube video:

  1. Step 1: Go to YouTube Studio
  2. Step 2: Click on "Content" and select your video
  3. Step 3: Scroll to the "Details" section
  4. Step 4: Add your main keywords in the title
  5. Step 5: Include keywords in the first 200 characters of your description
  6. Step 6: Add 5-10 tags (keywords) in the tags section
  7. Step 7: Save changes
