🏷️ 1. What are Channel Keywords on YouTube?

Channel keywords in YouTube are keywords that describe your channel's content. They help YouTube understand your niche and recommend your videos to the right audience. When you search for channel keywords YouTube meaning, it simply means the tags you add in your YouTube Studio settings.

➕ 3. How to Add Keywords to YouTube Channel

Wondering how to add keywords to your YouTube channel? Here is the exact step-by-step process:

  1. Go to YouTube Studio.
  2. Click on Settings (the gear icon) in the bottom left corner.
  3. Select Channel and then click on Basic info.
  4. Scroll down to the Keywords section.
  5. Type your keywords separated by commas (e.g., gaming, gaming tips, how to play).
  6. Click Save.

💡 Pro Tip: Always put your most important keyword first. YouTube gives more weight to the first few keywords.

📝 5. Where to Add Keywords in YouTube Video

Many creators ask where to add keywords in YouTube video. Here are the best places:

  • Title: Include your primary keyword in the first 60 characters.
  • Description: Add keywords naturally in the first 200 characters.
  • Tags: Add 10-15 relevant tags in the video settings.
  • Video File Name: Rename the video file before uploading (e.g., gaming-tips.mp4).
  • Chapters: Use keyword-rich titles for your timestamps.

🚫 7. How to Block Keywords in YouTube Search

A common question is how to block keywords in YouTube search. If you want to stop seeing certain videos, you can use YouTube's built-in feature:

  1. Go to YouTube Settings.
  2. Click on Manage all history.
  3. Go to YouTube History and click on Manage.
  4. Look for the Blocked content or similar option.

Alternatively, you can search for how to block keywords on YouTube on Google for browser extension solutions.
