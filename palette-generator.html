📊 1. Advanced Palette Modes

  • 🎨 Analogous: Colors next to each other on the color wheel — harmonious and pleasing
  • 🔄 Complementary: Opposite colors — high contrast and vibrant
  • 🔺 Triadic: Three evenly spaced colors — balanced and rich
  • ⚫ Monochromatic: Shades of a single color — clean and elegant
  • 🔲 Tetradic: Four colors forming a rectangle — diverse and complex
