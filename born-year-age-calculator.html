Generations Explained

Our calculator assigns each birth year to one of five commonly recognised generations:

  • Baby Boomer (1946–1964) — born after World War II during a period of rapid population growth.
  • Generation X (1965–1980) — the latchkey generation, raised during the rise of personal computers.
  • Millennial / Gen Y (1981–1996) — came of age around the turn of the millennium and the internet boom.
  • Generation Z (1997–2012) — the first truly digital-native generation.
  • Generation Alpha (2013–2024) — the newest generation, growing up with AI and smart devices.

If someone is born in 1965, they belong to Generation X. Born in 2000? Millennial. Born in 2010? Generation Z.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my age from my birth year?

Subtract your birth year from the target year. If you were born in 1965 and want to know your age in 2026, then 2026 − 1965 = 61. Our calculator also shows the age on January 1 (one year less if your birthday hasn't happened yet) and December 31 (always the full number).

Why are there two age results (Jan 1 and Dec 31)?

Because we don't know the exact birthday, the calculator shows both ends of the year. On January 1, your birthday may not have happened yet, so you're one year younger. By December 31, your birthday has definitely passed, so you're at the full age for that year.

Can I calculate age for any future year?

Yes. Simply change the "Target Year" field to any year up to 2200. For example, enter 2030 to see how old you'll be in that year.

How is the Chinese zodiac calculated?

Chinese zodiac animals follow a 12-year cycle based on birth year. The formula used is: (year − 4) mod 12. This gives a number from 0 to 11, which maps to Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, or Pig.

What generation am I if I was born in 1990?

If you were born in 1990, you're a Millennial (born between 1981 and 1996). The calculator automatically shows your generation label after each calculation.

Is this calculator accurate for any birth year?

Yes. It works for any birth year from 1900 to 2100, and any target year from 1900 to 2200. Results are calculated instantly in your browser.

Why does the age on Jan 1 show one year less?

Because Jan 1 is the very first day of the year, and unless someone was born exactly on January 1, their birthday hasn't happened yet. So their age is one year less than on December 31.

Is the Born Year Age Calculator free?

Yes, it is 100% free and requires no registration, sign-up, or download. All calculations happen directly in your browser.
