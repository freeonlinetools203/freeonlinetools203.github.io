📐 3. How Sheep Gestation Period Works

The sheep gestation period is the time from conception to birth. The standard formula is:

Lambing Date = Breeding Date + 147 Days

(Range: 142-152 days depending on breed and other factors)

Example:

  • Breeding Date: January 1, 2026
  • Gestation Period: 147 days
  • Lambing Date = May 28, 2026
