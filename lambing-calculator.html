🐑 Lambing Calculator — Sheep Gestation Date Calculator

A lambing calculator (also called sheep gestation calculator) helps sheep farmers and shepherds calculate the expected lambing date based on the breeding or mating date. Knowing the due date helps in proper planning, nutrition, and care for pregnant ewes.

🐑 What is a Lambing Calculator?

A lambing calculator is a tool that calculates the expected lambing date for sheep based on the breeding date. The average sheep gestation period is 147 days (ranging from 142-152 days). This tool uses that information to give you an accurate due date.

⚙️ How to Use This Lambing Calculator

  1. Select breed — choose your sheep breed or keep default
  2. Enter breeding date — select the mating date
  3. Click "Calculate Lambing Date" — see the due date instantly!

✅ Why Use This Lambing Calculator?

  • Accurate due dates — plan for lambing season
  • Multiple breeds — different gestation periods
  • Days countdown — see how many days left
  • Free to use — no registration required
  • Privacy first — all calculations in your browser
