📝 3. Step-by-Step: Calculate Age from Date of Birth in Excel

Follow these simple steps to calculate age from date of birth in Excel.

Step 1: Prepare Your Data

Open Excel and enter the date of birth in cell A2. Use a proper date format like 01/15/1990 or 15-Jan-1990.

Step 2: Use the DATEDIF Formula

In cell B2, type this formula:

=DATEDIF(A2, TODAY(), "Y")

This gives you the age in complete years.

Step 3: Get Age in Months and Days

For months: =DATEDIF(A2, TODAY(), "YM")
For days: =DATEDIF(A2, TODAY(), "MD")

Step 4: Combine for Full Age

Use this formula to show age as "35 Years, 2 Months, 10 Days":

=DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"Y") & " Years, " & DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"YM") & " Months, " & DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"MD") & " Days"

Step 5: Copy Formula Down

Drag the fill handle to apply the formula to all rows. Your Excel age calculation is complete.

❓ 9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: What is the formula to calculate age from date of birth in Excel?

Use =DATEDIF(A2, TODAY(), "Y") where A2 contains the date of birth. This returns age in complete years.

Q2: How do I calculate age in Excel in years, months, and days?

Use the combined formula: =DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"Y") & " Years, " & DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"YM") & " Months, " & DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"MD") & " Days"

Q3: Why is DATEDIF not showing in Excel's formula list?

DATEDIF is a hidden function in Excel. You can still use it by typing the formula manually. It works in all versions.

Q4: Can I calculate age in Google Sheets?

Yes! Google Sheets supports the same DATEDIF and TODAY functions. The formula is identical to Excel.

Q5: How do I calculate age from date of birth in Excel without TODAY()?

Replace TODAY() with a fixed date: =DATEDIF(A2, DATE(2026,1,1), "Y") to calculate age as of January 1, 2026.

Q6: What if my Excel shows a #NUM! error with DATEDIF?

This happens when the start date is greater than the end date. Ensure your date of birth is earlier than TODAY().

Q7: Can I calculate age in decimal years for statistical analysis?

Yes, use =YEARFRAC(A2, TODAY()) to get age in decimal years (e.g., 35.42).

Q8: How do I calculate date of birth from age in Excel?

Use =TODAY() - (A2 * 365.25) where A2 is the age in years. For more accuracy, use our dedicated Calculate Age From DOB in Excel guide.

🎯 10. Pro Tips & Troubleshooting

  • Tip 1: Format your result cells as "General" or "Number" — not "Date" — to avoid confusion.
  • Tip 2: Use =IF(A2="","",DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"Y")) to avoid errors on empty rows.
  • Tip 3: If DATEDIF doesn't auto-complete, type it manually — it's a hidden function but fully supported.
  • Tip 4: For international date formats, use DATEVALUE() to convert text dates before calculating.
  • Tip 5: Press Ctrl + Shift + ; to quickly insert today's date as a static value.
