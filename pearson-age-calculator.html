What is a Pearson Age Calculator?

A Pearson age calculator is a tool used to calculate chronological age for Pearson assessments and educational tests. Pearson assessments often require exact chronological age to determine appropriate test versions and score interpretations.

The Pearson chronological age calculator computes age in years, months, and days from the date of birth to the testing date. This is essential for accurate assessment scoring.

Our free online Pearson age calculator is designed to be simple and accurate, helping educators, psychologists, and test administrators calculate chronological age quickly and easily.
