📐 3. How to Calculate Pearson Age

How to calculate Pearson age is simple. The basic formula is:

Pearson Age = Testing Date – Date of Birth

The Pearson age calculator performs all three steps automatically:

  • Year Subtraction: Subtract the birth year from the current year.
  • Month Adjustment: If the current month is earlier than the birth month, borrow 12 months from the year difference.
  • Day Adjustment: If the current day is earlier than the birth day, borrow one month (using the number of days in the previous month).

💡 Example Calculation

Date of Birth: January 15, 1990

Testing Date: August 19, 2026

  • Years: 2026 - 1990 = 36 years
  • Months: August (8) - January (1) = 7 months
  • Days: 19 - 15 = 4 days
  • Result: 36 years, 7 months, 4 days

❓ 9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ What is a Pearson age calculator?
A Pearson age calculator computes a child's exact chronological age in Years/Months/Days format for use with Pearson assessments and standardized testing.
❓ Where is the official Pearson age calculator?
The official Pearson Chronological Age Calculator on Q-global was discontinued by Pearson in 2023. Pearson no longer hosts an active live age calculator on its primary assessment platforms.
❓ How do I calculate chronological age for Pearson assessments?
You can compute it by hand using the traditional borrow-30 method or use third-party digital chronological calculators designed for clinical evaluations.
❓ What is the Pearson Borrow-30 Method?
The Borrow-30 method stacks the test date above the birth date (Year/Month/Day) and subtracts from right to left. If the birth day is larger than the test day, you borrow 30 days from the months column. If the birth month is larger than the test month, you borrow 12 months from the years column.
❓ Why do general online calculators give different results than Pearson?
General web calculators use actual month lengths (varying between 28 and 31 days) or decimal years, whereas standardized clinical tests typically expect the uniform 30-day borrowing convention used by Pearson.

❓ How to calculate chronological age for testing?

❓ What is the chronological age calculator for Pearson assessments?

❓ How to calculate Pearson chronological age manually?
To calculate Pearson chronological age manually, use the borrow-30 method: write the test date above the birth date, subtract days (borrow 30 if needed), subtract months (borrow 12 if needed), then subtract years.
