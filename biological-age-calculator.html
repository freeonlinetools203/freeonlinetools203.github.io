What is Biological Age vs Chronological Age?

Chronological age is the number of years you've been alive. Biological age (also called physiological age) is a measure of how old your body seems based on various health and lifestyle factors.

Your biological age can be higher or lower than your chronological age. Healthy lifestyle choices like regular exercise, good diet, quality sleep, and avoiding smoking can lower your biological age.
