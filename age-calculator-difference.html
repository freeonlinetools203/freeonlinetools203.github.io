📐 2. How to Calculate Age Difference

How to calculate age difference between two dates:

Age Difference = Later Date – Earlier Date

The calculation involves three main steps:

  • Year Subtraction: Subtract the earlier year from the later year.
  • Month Adjustment: If the earlier month is greater than the later month, borrow 12 months.
  • Day Adjustment: If the earlier day is greater than the later day, borrow days from the previous month.

💡 Example Calculation

Date 1: January 15, 1990

Date 2: August 23, 2026

  • Years: 2026 - 1990 = 36 years
  • Months: August (8) - January (1) = 7 months
  • Days: 23 - 15 = 8 days
  • Result: 36 years, 7 months, 8 days

📊 3. Age Difference Formula

Component Formula Example

Months Later Month - Earlier Month 8 - 1 = 7
