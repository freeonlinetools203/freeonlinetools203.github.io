📊 1. What is a TDEE Calculator?

A TDEE calculator (Total Daily Energy Expenditure calculator) is a health assessment tool that estimates the total number of calories your body burns in a day. The total daily energy expenditure calculator provides a numerical value that represents your maintenance calories – the amount you need to eat to maintain your current weight.

Understanding what is a tdee calculator is essential for anyone serious about weight management. Whether how do i calculate my tdee for weight loss, muscle gain, or maintenance, our tool provides accurate results using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula with optional body fat adjustment.

📌 Did You Know? TDEE accounts for 60-75% of your total daily calorie burn from BMR alone, plus additional calories from physical activity and digestion.

📐 2. How to Calculate TDEE

How to calculate TDEE in two simple steps:

📌 Step 1: Calculate Your BMR

Men: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) + 5

Women: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) − 161

📌 Step 2: Multiply by Activity Level

  • Sedentary (1.2): Little or no exercise
  • Lightly Active (1.375): Light exercise 1-3 days/week
  • Moderately Active (1.55): Moderate exercise 3-5 days/week
  • Very Active (1.725): Hard exercise 6-7 days/week
  • Extra Active (1.9): Athlete or physical job

TDEE = BMR × Activity Factor

📊 3. TDEE Formula Explained

The TDEE formula uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, considered the most accurate for general populations:

Formula Male Female
Mifflin-St Jeor (10×W) + (6.25×H) - (5×A) + 5 (10×W) + (6.25×H) - (5×A) - 161
Katch-McArdle 370 + (21.6 × Lean Body Mass)

W = weight in kg, H = height in cm, A = age in years
