📊 Should You Use Hashtags on LinkedIn?

Many users ask, "do you use hashtags on LinkedIn?" or "should I use hashtags on LinkedIn?" The answer is a resounding YES. Here is why:

  • Algorithmic Reach: LinkedIn uses hashtags to understand what your post is about. If you use the best LinkedIn hashtags, your post will be shown to people who follow those tags, even if they aren't connected to you.
  • Targeted Networking: Using popular LinkedIn hashtags helps you connect with like-minded professionals. If you are in tech, using #ArtificialIntelligence or #TechNews places your content directly in front of tech enthusiasts.
  • Brand Authority: Consistently using top LinkedIn hashtags in your niche establishes you as a thought leader. When people search for those tags, your content will appear.
  • Job Opportunities: Using best LinkedIn hiring hashtags like #NowHiring or #JobOpening helps recruiters and job seekers find each other faster.

🔢 How Many Hashtags Should I Use on LinkedIn?

This is the most debated topic: how many hashtags should I use on LinkedIn? If you search online, you will find conflicting advice. However, based on LinkedIn's own recommendations and data from thousands of viral posts, here is the golden rule:

  • 3 to 5 Hashtags: This is the sweet spot. It provides enough categorization for the algorithm without looking spammy.
  • Maximum 8 Hashtags: You can use up to 8, but anything beyond 5-6 starts to dilute your reach.
  • Avoid 10+ Hashtags: LinkedIn is not Instagram. Using 20-30 hashtags on LinkedIn will likely get your post flagged as spam, and the algorithm will suppress it.

If you want to know how to use hashtags on LinkedIn effectively, the formula is simple: Use 1 Broad tag (e.g., #Marketing), 2-3 Niche tags (e.g., #ContentMarketing, #B2BMarketing), and 1 Trending/Community tag (e.g., #MarketingStrategy).

📈 LinkedIn Hashtag Analytics: How to Track Your Success

Using a LinkedIn hashtag analytics approach is vital if you want to know which tags are working. You don't necessarily need a paid LinkedIn hashtag analytics extension to see results. Here is how you can track them manually:

  • Check Impressions: Look at the analytics of your post. If a post with a specific hashtag got 50% more views than usual, that hashtag works.
  • Follower Growth: See if people are finding your profile after you use specific tags.
  • The "Follow" Button: Go to LinkedIn, search for a hashtag, and see how many followers it has. If a hashtag has millions of followers, it's too competitive. If it has 10k-50k, it's a great niche tag.

➕ How to Add Hashtags to LinkedIn Posts

Many users ask, how to add hashtags to LinkedIn posts? It is extremely simple. Follow these steps:

  1. Write your post: Create your text, image, or video post as usual.
  2. Type the '#' symbol: Start typing the symbol followed by your keyword (no spaces). For example, #Marketing.
  3. Select from dropdown: LinkedIn will suggest existing hashtags. Always select from the dropdown to ensure you are using an active, followed hashtag.
  4. CamelCase is Key: Always capitalize the first letter of each word (e.g., #DigitalMarketing, not #digitalmarketing). This makes it easier to read and is better for accessibility.
  5. Place them at the bottom: Put your 3-5 hashtags at the very end of your post. Do not clutter your main text with them.
